## Per-slide checks

- The title is a sentence, and the exhibit is its evidence.
- One part of the exhibit is highlighted: the part the title names.
- Presented mode: no paragraph on screen that the speaker reads out; the sentences live in notes.
- Read-alone mode: the slide is understood with no voice; otherwise write a one-pager instead.
- Every chart has its source, unit and period; every image has a text alternative.
- The same thing has the same name and the same colour on every slide.
